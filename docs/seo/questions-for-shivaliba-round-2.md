The pages now say "go to A&E now, or call NHS 111 if you are not sure where to go", which is more urgent than the NHS pages. Is that right?

3. **Septic arthritis in the shoulder: "and" or "or"?** NICE lists a red, hot, painful joint OR a fever or feeling unwell. Our shoulder page currently asks for both together. Which should it be?

4. **Children at home visits.** We now say that for anyone under 18, a parent or guardian must be present for the whole home visit. This is a new commitment in our terms. Are you happy with it?

5. **Home address records.** We keep the home address with the appointment records for 12 months, or with the clinical notes for 8 years if you write it into them. Is that right for your practice?

6. **Early post-op exercises in the library.** The exercise library still shows early, protected-phase exercises for after surgery. They are now labelled "for reference only". Should the library show them at all, given your answer about starting only once there are no restrictions?
