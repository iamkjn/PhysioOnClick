# Competitor Keyword & Gap Analysis — PhysioOnClick

**Date:** 2026-09-30 · **Status:** for owner review, nothing built yet
**Raw evidence:** [competitors-50-raw-2026-09-30.md](competitors-50-raw-2026-09-30.md) (per-competitor keyword lists) · [serp-sampling-raw-2026-09-30.md](serp-sampling-raw-2026-09-30.md) (71 Google-style searches, who ranks)

### How this was measured (read first)
- No paid keyword tool is connected (Ahrefs / Semrush / Similarweb need authorising), so **there are no search-volume numbers**. "Successful keywords" were worked out from evidence:
  - (a) the competitor showed up in real search results for the query (**SEEN**);
  - (b) their page titles and URLs are clearly built for it (**INFERRED**).
- The search tool runs from the US. Treat competitor rankings as a guide to google.co.uk, not exact positions.
- **Our own data is real.** It comes from Google Search Console, 90 days to 29 Sep 2026: 1,074 impressions across 500 queries.

## 1. Headline findings

1. **We are the cheapest credible online physio in the UK and we don't say so where it matters.** Our prices are £40 for the initial assessment and £30 for a follow-up. Competitors charge:

   | Competitor | Initial / follow-up |
   |---|---|
   | Ascenti | £44 (video session) |
   | PromoteHealth | £45 / £40 |
   | PhysioFast | £65 / £47 |
   | Nuffield | £72 / £49 |
   | Complete Physio | £125 / £95 |

   Most online winners put the price in their title or H1. Price and cost queries are won by small clinic blogs and comparison sites, which makes them winnable.
2. **"Online physio for [condition]" is the biggest open gap.** For sciatica, back, neck, shoulder and plantar fasciitis, the ranking pages are an app aggregator (resolve360) and London clinic pages that aren't really online. No strong online-only provider owns these. We have a condition hub (`/exercises/for/*`), but it is built around *exercises*, not *"online physio for X"*.
3. **Our exercise library is already our best SEO asset.** It ranks on page 1 in Search Console:

   | Query | Position |
   |---|---|
   | balance one leg eyes closed | 2.4 |
   | mckenzie push up | 3.0 |
   | hip hitch | 3.3 |
   | isometric neck hold | 6.8 |
   | chin retraction | 8.9 |
   | ankle pumps after surgery | 10.1 |

   "knee replacement physio" is at 3.9. It isn't converting, though (clicks ≈ 0).
4. **There is real demand for neuro physio in Glasgow and we're sitting on page 2–3.** About 15 variants of "neurological physiotherapy glasgow", "neuro physio glasgow" and "fnd physiotherapy glasgow" add up to roughly 200 impressions at positions 19–28. None is a local-pack query in practice, and small neuro specialists (SGNP, Movement Physio, Three Spires) win them. This is the single biggest pool in our own data.
5. **Head terms and local-pack terms are still off the table.** "physio glasgow", "physiotherapist near me" and bare "physiotherapy" are local-pack or big-brand territory, and we can't have a Business Profile because we're online-only. Nothing in this research changes that.
6. **Side finding (security / brand):** the GitHub repo `iamkjn/PhysioOnClick` is **public** and ranks **#2 for our brand name**. The summary Google shows for it describes "in-person care in Glasgow", which contradicts our positioning, and it exposes technical detail. Recommend making it private.

## 4. Missing points (us vs competitors)

**Pages we don't have that competitors rank with**
1. **"Online physio for [condition]" landing pages (C3).** These are service-intent pages, not exercise pages. Start with sciatica, low back pain, neck pain, shoulder, knee OA, plantar fasciitis, tennis elbow and hip.
2. **Cost and insurance guides (C4).** Topics to cover:
   - "How much does private physio cost in the UK (2026)"
   - "Can I claim online physio on Bupa/AXA/Vitality?" (use our HCPC receipt)
   - "Do I need a GP referral?"
   - "How many sessions will I need?"
   - "NHS physio waiting times in Scotland vs going private"
3. **Neuro depth (C8).** We have one neuro service page. Competitors have one page per condition: stroke, MS, Parkinson's, FND, brain injury. Google already tests us for "neuro physio glasgow"; we need an "online neurological physiotherapy for Glasgow & Scotland" page and FND/stroke/Parkinson's sub-pages.
4. **"Does online physio work?" answer pages (C2).** These are short cited Q&A pages, the kind AI Overviews quote:
   - PEAK trial
   - What can't be done over video
   - Can a physio diagnose over video
5. **Online physio Scotland page (C12).**
6. **Women's / pelvic health (C11).** This needs the owner's decision on whether to offer it.
7. **"Best online physio UK" comparison page (C14).** Honest and factual, with our price advantage stated.

**Things competitors do on-page that we don't**
- **Price in title / H1 / meta** ("Online Physio from £40"). Nearly every online winner does this.
- **Insurer names** ("receipts accepted by Bupa, AXA, Vitality, Aviva") on the pricing and service pages. Say it only once the owner confirms it's true.
- **Reviews count in the snippet.** Competitors show "4.9 ★ 2,145 reviews". We now have 3 Trustpilot reviews; add review schema once there are more.
- **NHS-wait contrast** ("NHS GGC ~17 weeks → see a physio this week").
- **A self-assessment tool in the main nav** (PhysioFast, Peter's body map). We have self-tests and an assessment body chart, but neither is surfaced in the nav.

**Pages that already rank but don't convert**
- The exercise pages rank on page 1 with ~0 clicks. Improve titles/meta so they earn the click (e.g. "Isometric Neck Hold — how-to, sets & reps (physio-written)").
- Add a "Want a physio to check your form? Online assessment £40" call to action on each page.
