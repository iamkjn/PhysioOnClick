# Outreach — free exercise embeds

_Drafted 2026-10-02. Goal: get local gyms, studios, clubs and complementary clinics to embed our exercises (or link to them) on their websites. Every embed carries a credit link back to physioonclick.co.uk, and each one is a backlink._

---

## How to send (read once)

1. **Send from hello@physioonclick.co.uk**, signed by Shivaliba. A real person's name gets far more replies than a brand.
2. **Personalise the first line every time.** Mention something real about them (a class, a race they host, a recent post). Generic emails get ignored.
3. **Use their business address or contact form**, not a staff member's personal email. UK rules (PECR) allow business-to-business emails to company addresses, but sole traders count as individuals, so for a one-person business use their website contact form instead.
4. **Keep the opt-out line** at the bottom of each email. It's a legal requirement for marketing emails and it builds trust.
5. **5–10 emails a week**, not all at once. Follow up **once** after 7 days if there's no reply, then stop.
6. **Log every send** in the tracker at the bottom. When someone embeds, note the page URL and tell me. I'll check the link is live.

**Who NOT to email:** other physiotherapy clinics (competitors, won't link), big chains like PureGym and The Gym Group (central marketing teams, local staff can't change the website), and NHS GP practices (they generally can't promote private providers).
