# Why Home Physio And Rehab Ltd gets Glasgow enquiries and we don't (2026-11-23)

Checked live in Google (UK, from Glasgow), on their site, and in our Search Console data
(25 Aug – 21 Nov 2026).

## Who they are

- A London company (Harrow, HA2), incorporated 30 Jan 2026, domain registered 2 Feb 2026.
  Our domain was registered 12 Jul 2026, so they have a five-month head start.
- No Glasgow premises. They sub-contract self-employed physios (job ads for Paisley/Scotland
  on Indeed and Jobrapido, Nov 2026). Their Glasgow page says so openly.

Our on-page wording is already right ("Home Physiotherapy Glasgow", prices in titles), so
wording is not the gap. Most of their reviews are from London patients, and a block of them are
one-line "Simply 5 Stars!" reviews, but Google still counts them.

## Map pack (the three businesses above the website results)

For "home physio glasgow", "physio home visits paisley" and "home physiotherapy hamilton", a
home-visit physio with only **6 Google reviews** ("Specialist Home Physiotherapy") appears in
the top three. A verified Google Business Profile with a handful of real reviews is enough
to get in. Ours is still unverified, so we can't appear there at all.

## What we changed (branch feat/glasgow-town-pages)

- 7 town pages at /physiotherapy-home-visits/<town>: Paisley, East Kilbride, Hamilton,
  Motherwell & Wishaw, Clydebank, Rutherglen & Cambuslang, Kilmarnock. Each has its own
  postcodes, neighbourhoods, nearby towns, local hospital (where sources agree) and its own
  questions. Prices, hours and the signed-off clinical wording are shared, never retyped.
- Real Trustpilot reviews now also on the Glasgow home-visit page and every town page.
- Town links from /physiotherapy-home-visits-glasgow and /glasgow-physiotherapist; sitemap.

## What only the owner can do (in order of impact)

1. Record the Google Business Profile verification video. Without it we're invisible in the map pack.
2. Once verified, ask real patients for Google reviews (not family or friends).
3. Fill the empty Facebook page and list the practice on the CSP "Find a physio" directory,
   Nextdoor, Yell and Bing Places (the citations sheet is docs/seo/glasgow-citations-sheet.md).
4. Post regularly (Facebook/Instagram), as they do. Brand mentions are what Google and ChatGPT use.

Rule: never buy, swap or write reviews. Their review pattern is not something to copy.
