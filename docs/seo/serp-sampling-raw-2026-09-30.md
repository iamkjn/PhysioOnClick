# PhysioOnClick SERP research (2026-09-30)

## Method and caveats (read first)
- 71 queries run through the WebSearch tool. **The tool is US-located**, so it returns an approximation of a UK SERP. Adding "uk" to a query pulls UK sites back in. Bare condition queries such as "neck pain exercises" come back US-heavy (WebMD, Cleveland Clinic, Hinge Health, Kaiser). A real google.co.uk SERP would show NHS / nhsinform / Versus Arthritis / CSP much higher.
- The tool returns about 9 organic links per query in rank-like order. It **does not expose SERP features**: no People Also Ask box, video carousel, local pack or AI Overview. Section (c) says what can and cannot be inferred.
- "Present?" means physioonclick.co.uk appeared anywhere in the returned links.
- Page types used: SP = service/landing page, CG = condition guide, BL = blog post, DIR = directory/aggregator, NHS = NHS/nhsinform/trust leaflet, APP = app/app store, TOOL = checker/quiz, VID = video, RES = research (PMC/Lancet/trials), CHAR = charity (Versus Arthritis, Parkinson's UK, BHF, Age UK), PDF = exercise sheet.

## Synthesis

### (a) Domains appearing most often
**Overall (count of queries each domain appeared in, roughly):**
- NHS family (nhs.uk, nhsinform.scot, NHS trusts, nhsggc.scot, 111.wales): **~16 queries**. This is by far the largest bloc, and it is under-counted here because the tool is US-located.
- ncbi/pmc + clinicaltrials + Lancet (research): ~12
- resolve360.app: **8** (an app/aggregator using a "UK's No.1" page, "top 10" listicle and condition pages to rank on almost every online+condition query)
- physiomantra.co: 6 (a directory/aggregator with city and condition pages)
- physio.co.uk: 6
- onebodyldn.com: 6 (a London clinic with insurance, price, condition and "NHS vs private" blogs, ranking across cost, insurance and knee queries)
- central-health.com: 5
- nuffieldhealth.com: 5
- complete-physio.co.uk: 5
- southgatephysio.co.uk: 5
- kaiserpermanente / healthline / hingehealth / clevelandclinic: 4-5 each (US artefact on bare exercise queries)
- true.physio: 4; physio-pedia: 6; sports-injury-physio.com: 4; goingprivateuk.co.uk: 4; excellencephysiotherapy.com: 4

**By cluster (leaders):**
1. Online category: central-health, teladochealth, zoomphysio, nuffieldhealth, southgatephysio, cornerstonephysio, proudphysio. **physioonclick appears on 2 of 9.**
2. Cost/insurance: true.physio, complete-physio, onebodyldn, goingprivateuk, treatcompare/privatecarecompare, mytribeinsurance.
3. Bare condition: NHS/nhsinform, BHF, Versus Arthritis, plus US health publishers.
4. Condition+online: resolve360, physiomantra, physio.co.uk, London clinics. **No provider owns "online physio for X".**
5. Post-surgery: small home-visit clinics (fouroaks, southgate, threespires, londonhomevisit), plus US hospitals for bare "exercises".
6. Exercises: NHS trust PDFs, arthritis-uk, hospital PDFs, fitness DBs (musclewiki, workoutlabs, physitrack).
7. Symptom checker: US hospitals/blogs; the UK tool slot goes to NHS 111 Wales, NHFT and providephysiotherapy.
8. Neuro/paeds/older adults: charities (Parkinson's UK, Age UK, BGS) and small specialist neuro clinics.
9. Glasgow: Nuffield, Peter's Physiotherapy, Glasgow Medical Rooms, Berkley Kemp, Circle (Ross Hall), fresha/yelp directories.
10. Brand: Trustpilot/Glassdoor, Bupa, app stores, Ascenti.

### (b) Difficulty: hard vs winnable
**Hard (NHS / big brands / charities dominate):**
- Bare condition exercise queries ("tennis elbow exercises", "achilles tendonitis exercises", "knee arthritis exercises", "chair exercises elderly"). NHS trust leaflets take up to 7 of 9 slots, plus Versus Arthritis and BHF.
- "parkinsons exercises", "falls prevention": Parkinson's UK, Age UK, BGS and research.
- "physio app uk": app stores plus NHS Phio/GetUBetter.
- Brand/review queries for Nuffield and Bupa.
- "physio glasgow" / "physiotherapist glasgow": local-pack driven, and PhysioOnClick can't compete there (not GBP-eligible).
- "nhs physio waiting times": NHS data pages.

**Winnable (small or independent sites clearly ranking):**
- **Online-physio category** ("online physiotherapy uk", "online physio appointment", "video physiotherapy consultation uk"): small clinics like southgatephysio, gatleyheatons and atherton rank, and PhysioOnClick already appears on 2 of these.
- **Online-physio question/education content** ("does online physiotherapy work", "online vs in person", "can a physio diagnose over video call"): won by one-person blogs such as proudphysio, cornerstonephysio and sports-injury-physio.com. PhysioOnClick has no page ranking here yet.
