# PhysioOnClick: Competitor Keyword Research (50 competitors)

Researched 2026-09-30. There was no paid keyword tool, so everything here comes from public evidence: WebSearch SERPs (note: the search tool is US-hosted, so rankings are only a rough stand-in for google.co.uk) and WebFetch of homepages and service pages.

**Legend**
- **SEEN**: this domain appeared in the search results for that query (or a very close variant) during this research.
- **INFERRED**: the page's title, H1 or URL targets it, but I didn't see it rank.
- No search volumes are given, because I had no tool to measure them.
- Where a site blocked fetching (403), the evidence comes from SERP titles and snippets. These are marked (403).

## Gaps and opportunities for PhysioOnClick (from the evidence)
1. **Online + Scotland/Glasgow**: few pure online providers target it. Physiotherapy Scotland, PhysioFast and Southgate rank for "online physiotherapy Scotland" with thin pages.
2. **Online neuro and online paediatric** SERPs are held by small niche sites (SGNP, Three Spires, Movement, Baby Physio) and a charity. They are winnable with question-style H2s.
3. **Post-surgical rehab online**: this SERP is all home-visit and London sites plus Physio.co.uk's what-we-treat tree. There is no strong online player.
4. **Exercise plans**: Pure Physiotherapy's /exercise-plans/ and NHS trust PDFs rank for condition exercise queries, which our exercise library could contest.
5. **Price transparency**: most online winners show prices (£40–£125) on the service page itself.
6. **Self-assessment tools** (PhysioFast MSK self-assessment, Peter's body map) are used as low-friction entry points.
7. **Evidence pages** ("is online physio as effective", REFORM trial): Ascenti owns this query with a report page.
8. **Price-cost informational queries** are held by aggregators, not providers.
