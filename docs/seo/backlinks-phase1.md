**Before you start:**

## Tier 1 — Professional bodies (highest trust, do first)

### 1. CSP Physio2u practice listing ⭐ start here
- **What:** The Chartered Society of Physiotherapy's public "find a physio" service. Shivaliba is already a CSP member (no. 128230), and her member profile is live.
- **Cost:** The basic listing is free. **Premium is £49 + VAT a year, and that's the version that includes a website link**, so pay for premium. The basic listing gives no backlink.
- **How:** Log in at csp.org.uk → Your Account → View account → "Your practice listing" → "Add your practice". Choose the clinical specialities (MSK, post-surgical rehab, neuro rehab, paediatrics) and mention online consultations.
- **Guide:** https://www.csp.org.uk/content/your-practice-physio2u-how-manage-your-entry
- **Help:** enquiries@csp.org.uk

### 2. Physio First "Find a Physio"
- **What:** The CSP network for self-employed physios in private practice. The directory has an **online consultation** filter, which suits us exactly.
- **Cost:** Paid membership (Individual route). Check the current price on the join page. Membership also brings practice support and outcome data, so it's not just a link.
- **Join:** https://www.physiofirst.org.uk/join-physio-first.html
- **Directory:** https://www.physiofirst.org.uk/find-physio.html

## Tier 2 — Health insurers (slow, but brings patients as well as trust)

Insurer "find a provider" pages don't always link to your website, so treat these mainly as a way to reach **insured patients**, with the link as a bonus. The applications take weeks, so start them early.

### 3. Healthcode Private Practice Register (do before Aviva)
- Aviva manages provider registration through Healthcode. AXA also requires claims to go through Healthcode, so registering once here unlocks both.
- https://www.healthcode.co.uk

### 4. AXA Health — individual recognition
- **Eligibility:** at least **1 year of full HCPC registration** (PH155757). Check the registration date on the HCPC register.
- AXA opens applications by specialty at set times, so check that physiotherapy is open before gathering paperwork.
- https://provider.axahealth.co.uk/individual-provider-recognition/

### 5. Aviva
- Apply via the Healthcode Private Practice Register (step 3). HCPC registration is required.

### 6. Bupa Physiotherapy Network ⚠️ check eligibility first
- Bupa needs a lead clinician with **5 years' full-time post-qualification experience**, plus CSP + HCPC registration, an enhanced DBS and indemnity insurance. **If Shivaliba doesn't meet 5 years yet, skip this for now** and diary it.
- https://www.bupa.co.uk/healthcare-professionals/for-your-role/therapists/physio-network
- Enquiries: 0345 600 5422 (Mon–Fri, 10am–4pm)

### 7. Vitality (Priority Physio network)
- No public application page. Phone or email Vitality's provider team and ask how to join the Priority Physio panel as an online physio. Low priority.

## Tier 3 — Health directories

### 8. Doctify
- Free basic profile. Search Doctify for "Shivaliba Zala" first, because a profile may already exist that just needs claiming. If there isn't one, create one.
- Doctify verification is built around GMC/GDC, so **check they accept HCPC registration** before spending time on it.
- https://www.doctify.com/uk

### 9. Find a Physio (findaphysio.uk) — optional
- New and small. Free for 6 months, then £49.99 a year. It supports "Online" as a delivery option.
- Low value today. Only do it if it's quick, and cancel before the paid year starts unless it's sending enquiries.
- https://www.findaphysio.uk/
