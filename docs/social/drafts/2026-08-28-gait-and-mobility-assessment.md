## 1. Quick Source Scan

- NICE, "Falls: assessment and prevention in older people and in people 50 and over at higher risk" NG249. Published and last reviewed 29 April 2025. Recent UK guideline. Relevant point: falls prevention should include identifying people at risk, comprehensive falls assessment and interventions to reduce falls risk. https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng249
- NHS, "Physical activity guidelines for older adults". Page last reviewed 15 August 2024. Evergreen. Relevant point: older adults should do activities that improve strength, balance and flexibility on at least 2 days a week; people who have fallen or worry about falling may benefit from strength, balance and flexibility exercise. https://www.nhs.uk/live-well/exercise/physical-activity-guidelines-older-adults/
- NHS, "Keep bones healthy over 65". Page last reviewed 31 October 2023; includes falls and mobility considerations and a timed up and go video last reviewed 30 September 2024. Evergreen. Relevant point: foot health, eyesight, hearing, medicines and home safety can affect mobility and falls risk. https://www.nhs.uk/live-well/bone-health/keep-your-bones-strong-over-65/
- NICE, "Stroke rehabilitation in adults" NG236. Published 18 October 2023. Evergreen. Relevant point: stroke rehabilitation is needs-based and should include personalised goals, mobility, activities of daily living and multidisciplinary support. https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng236
- Kingston and Richmond NHS Foundation Trust, "Home Exercise Programme: Falls Prevention". Last updated 4 August 2026. Genuinely recent NHS Trust patient resource. Relevant point: strength and balance programmes are used in falls prevention, with referral/supervision where appropriate. https://www.kingstonandrichmond.nhs.uk/patients-and-families/patient-leaflets/home-exercise-programme-falls-prevention
