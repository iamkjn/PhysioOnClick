## 1. Quick Source Scan

- NHS, "Physiotherapy". Page last reviewed 3 April 2025. Current NHS patient information. Relevant point: a first physiotherapy appointment usually includes questions about symptoms, medical history and lifestyle, plus checks such as strength and balance before treatment options are discussed. https://www.nhs.uk/tests-and-treatments/physiotherapy/
- Chartered Society of Physiotherapy, "Digital, print and virtual physiotherapy". Last reviewed 29 June 2026. Genuinely recent professional guidance. Relevant point: specific physiotherapy advice should be based on individual assessment and the physiotherapist must judge whether remote care is suitable. https://www.csp.org.uk/professional-clinical/professional-guidance/insurance/digital-virtual-physiotherapy
- Chartered Society of Physiotherapy, "Remote consultations". Current professional guidance page, accessed 11 September 2026. Relevant point: remote physiotherapy can be used for triage, assessment, rehabilitation support, self-management information and monitoring where appropriate. https://www.csp.org.uk/professional-clinical/professional-guidance/remote-consultations
- HCPC, "Standards of proficiency - Physiotherapists". Effective from 1 September 2023. UK regulator source. Relevant point: physiotherapists must gather appropriate information, undertake assessment, use evidence-informed practice and evaluate intervention plans. https://www.hcpc-uk.org/standards/standards-of-proficiency/physiotherapists/
- CSP, "Choose Chartered". Last reviewed 20 August 2024. Professional body source. Relevant point: UK physiotherapists must be registered with the HCPC, and chartered physiotherapists are members of the CSP. https://www.csp.org.uk/public-patient/find-physiotherapist/why-chartered-physiotherapist
- ASA/CAP, "Health: Therapies (General)". Published 2 March 2022. UK advertising guidance. Relevant point: health therapy claims should be substantiated, must not mislead, and should not discourage essential medical treatment. https://www.asa.org.uk/advice-online/health-therapies-general.html
