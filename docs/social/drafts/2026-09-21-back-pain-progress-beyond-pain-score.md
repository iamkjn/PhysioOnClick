## 1. Quick Source Scan

- NHS, ["Back pain"](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/back-pain/), last reviewed 5 March 2026. Evergreen patient guidance: staying active and continuing daily activities is generally encouraged, with medical review if pain worsens or serious symptoms develop.
- NICE, ["Low back pain and sciatica in over 16s" NG59](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng59/chapter/recommendations), first published 30 November 2016; recommendations amended in 2020. Evergreen guidance: support should be tailored, include encouragement to continue normal activities, and consider return to daily life and work.
- CSP, ["Musculoskeletal physiotherapy service standards"](https://www.csp.org.uk/professional-clinical/improvement-innovation/musculoskeletal-service-standards/msk-physio-standards), published and last reviewed 27 January 2022. Evergreen professional standard: MSK goals and reviews should reflect the person's needs, preferences and functional roles, with appropriate outcome measures.
- CSP, ["Outcome and experience measures"](https://www.csp.org.uk/professional-clinical/research-evaluation/outcome-experience-measures), last reviewed 15 May 2025. Evergreen professional guidance: outcome measures consider the person's perspective, including mobility, usual activities and pain or discomfort.
- HCPC, ["Standards of proficiency - Physiotherapists"](https://www.hcpc-uk.org/standards/standards-of-proficiency/physiotherapists/), effective 1 September 2023. Evergreen regulator standard: goals should be agreed with the person; intervention plans should be evaluated and revised when needed.

No relevant new clinical recommendation from the last 30 days was confirmed. One NHS trust page labels a linked back-pain leaflet "revised Sept 2026", but the linked PDF itself says it was printed in 2019 with review due in 2021, so it is not treated as a new source.
