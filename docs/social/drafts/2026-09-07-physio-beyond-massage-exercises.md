## 1. Quick Source Scan

- World Physiotherapy, "World PT Day 2026 materials launched". Published 8 June 2026. Current campaign source. Relevant point: World Physiotherapy Day 2026 is on 8 September and focuses on the role of physiotherapy and physical activity in prevention, management and rehabilitation of cardiovascular disease and stroke. https://world.physio/news/world-pt-day-2026-materials-launched
- NHS, "Physiotherapy". Page last reviewed 3 April 2025. Current NHS patient information. Relevant point: physiotherapy can help with injury, illness or disability and may involve exercise, manual therapy, education, advice and support for movement, strength, balance and recovery after surgery or stroke. https://www.nhs.uk/tests-and-treatments/physiotherapy/
- Chartered Society of Physiotherapy, "What is physiotherapy?" Last reviewed 7 March 2023. Professional body source. Relevant point: physiotherapy helps people affected by injury, illness or disability through movement and exercise, manual therapy, education and advice, taking a whole-person approach. https://www.csp.org.uk/careers-jobs/what-physiotherapy
- HCPC, "Standards of proficiency - Physiotherapists". Page updated 1 September 2023. UK regulator source. Relevant point: physiotherapists must gather and evaluate information, perform thorough assessment, use evidence to inform practice and deliver safe, effective care. https://www.hcpc-uk.org/standards/standards-of-proficiency/physiotherapists/
- Chartered Society of Physiotherapy, "Choose Chartered". Last reviewed 20 August 2024. Professional body source. Relevant point: UK physiotherapists must be HCPC registered to use the protected title physiotherapist, and chartered physiotherapists commit to professional standards and evidence-informed practice. https://www.csp.org.uk/public-patient/find-physiotherapist/why-chartered-physiotherapist
