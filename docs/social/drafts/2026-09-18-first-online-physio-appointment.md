## 1. Quick Source Scan

- NHS, ["Physiotherapy"](https://www.nhs.uk/tests-and-treatments/physiotherapy/), last reviewed 3 April 2025. Evergreen patient guidance: a first appointment includes questions about symptoms, medical history and lifestyle; movement, strength or balance may be checked as appropriate.
- NICE, ["Shared decision making" NG197](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng197/chapter/recommendations), published 17 June 2021. Evergreen guidance: people should be able to explain what matters to them, ask questions and agree next steps with a professional, including in remote consultations.
- CSP, ["Remote consultations"](https://www.csp.org.uk/professional-clinical/professional-guidance/remote-consultations), current professional guidance accessed 18 September 2026; no update date displayed. Remote physiotherapy can support assessment and rehabilitation when appropriate to the person and purpose.
- HCPC, ["Standards of proficiency - Physiotherapists"](https://www.hcpc-uk.org/standards/standards-of-proficiency/physiotherapists/), effective 1 September 2023. Evergreen regulator standard: physiotherapists must assess, use clinical reasoning and refer when needed.
- ASA/CAP, ["Health: Therapies (General)"](https://www.asa.org.uk/advice-online/health-therapies-general.html), published 2 March 2022. Advertising claims about therapies must be supported and must not discourage essential medical treatment.

No directly relevant new clinical guidance from the past 30 days was identified in this scan. This is an evergreen preparation topic, not a news claim.
