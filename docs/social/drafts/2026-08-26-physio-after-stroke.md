## 1. Quick Source Scan

- NICE, "Stroke rehabilitation in adults" NG236. Published 18 October 2023; current NICE guideline. Evergreen. Relevant point: stroke rehabilitation should be needs-based and delivered by specialist stroke services with multidisciplinary input; therapy goals should be tailored to the person. https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng236
- NICE NG236 recommendations. Evergreen. Relevant point: rehab planning includes mobility, activities of daily living, communication, cognition, mood and participation; telerehabilitation may be considered when it is safe, effective and acceptable for the person. https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng236/chapter/Recommendations
- NHS, "Stroke - Recovery". Page last reviewed 12 September 2024; next review due 12 September 2027. Evergreen. Relevant point: rehabilitation can involve physiotherapy, occupational therapy, speech and language therapy and psychological support; recovery varies between people. https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/stroke/recovery/
- NHS, "Stroke - Symptoms". Page last reviewed 12 September 2024; next review due 12 September 2027. Safety point: sudden face, arm or speech symptoms require emergency action via 999. https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/stroke/symptoms/
- NHS Highland Right Decisions, "Exercise After Stroke Pathway". Last reviewed 4 December 2025; updated 5 December 2025. Genuinely recent UK/Scottish pathway. Relevant point: exercise after stroke should consider safety, capacity, support needs and progression. https://rightdecisions.scot.nhs.uk/exercise-after-stroke-pathway/
