## 1. Quick Source Scan

- NICE, "Joint replacement (primary): hip, knee and shoulder" NG157. Current NICE guidance for planned hip, knee and shoulder replacement pathways. Evergreen. Relevant point: rehabilitation, discharge planning, follow-up and shared decision-making should be planned around the person and procedure. https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng157
- NHS, "Hip replacement - recovery". Current NHS patient guidance. Evergreen. Relevant points: recovery varies between people, follow physiotherapy advice, use walking aids as advised, and seek help for concerning symptoms such as increasing pain, swelling, redness or discharge. https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/hip-replacement/recovery/
- NHS, "Knee replacement - recovery". Current NHS patient guidance. Evergreen. Relevant points: physiotherapy exercises and gradual activity progression support recovery after knee replacement; patients should follow the care team's advice and seek help for signs of complications. https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/knee-replacement/recovery/
- NHS, "Anterior cruciate ligament (ACL) surgery - recovery". Current NHS patient guidance. Evergreen. Relevant point: ACL rehabilitation is staged and can take months, with progression depending on pain, swelling, range, strength and function. https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/anterior-cruciate-ligament-acl-surgery/recovery/
- Chartered Society of Physiotherapy public exercise safety advice. Evergreen safety point: seek advice from a physiotherapist or healthcare professional before starting a new exercise regime, and seek urgent advice if exercises worsen pain. https://www.csp.org.uk/conditions/back-pain/video-exercises-back-pain
