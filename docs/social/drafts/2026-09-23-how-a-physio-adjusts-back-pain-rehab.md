## 1. Quick Source Scan

- NHS, ["Back pain"](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/back-pain/), last reviewed 5 March 2026. Evergreen patient guidance: people are generally encouraged to remain active, but should stop an exercise and seek advice if pain worsens; severe or neurological symptoms need urgent assessment.
- NICE, ["Low back pain and sciatica in over 16s" NG59](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng59/chapter/recommendations), published 30 November 2016 and amended in 2020. Evergreen guidance: information and support should be tailored to the person's needs and capabilities, and exercise selection should consider their needs, preferences and capabilities.
- CSP, ["Musculoskeletal physiotherapy service standards"](https://www.csp.org.uk/professional-clinical/improvement-innovation/musculoskeletal-service-standards/msk-physio-standards), published and last reviewed 27 January 2022. Evergreen professional standard: assessment, planning and review should respond to the person's needs, preferences and goals and use appropriate outcome measures.
- CSP, ["MSK service standards - patient version"](https://www.csp.org.uk/professional-clinical/improvement-innovation/musculoskeletal-service-standards/msk-standards-patient), published and last reviewed 16 May 2022. Evergreen patient-facing standard: a physiotherapy plan should be tailored to personal needs and lifestyle, with the person involved in decisions.
- HCPC, ["Standards of proficiency - Physiotherapists"](https://www.hcpc-uk.org/standards/standards-of-proficiency/physiotherapists/), effective 1 September 2023. Evergreen regulator standard: physiotherapists evaluate care plans with the person where possible and revise them when circumstances or health status change.

No useful new clinical guidance from the last 30 days was confirmed for this specific topic. This pack therefore uses established UK guidance and makes no news claim.
