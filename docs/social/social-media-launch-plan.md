## Weekly Rhythm

Post three core short-form videos each week. Repurpose each video across Instagram Reels, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts with platform-specific captions.

- Monday 12:15: education or current guidance
- Wednesday 18:30: treatment/protocol explainer
- Friday 12:15: trust, booking, service, or offer post

Instagram Stories:

- 2 to 4 per week
- Use polls, question boxes, availability reminders, quick myth checks, and behind-the-scenes clips.

## Content Pillars

1. Awareness: back pain, joint pain, surgery recovery, balance, stroke recovery, paediatric movement, online rehab.
2. Evidence: NHS, NICE, GOV.UK, CSP, HCPC, World Physiotherapy, and peer-reviewed sources where relevant.
3. Treatment Journey: what happens in assessment, how rehab is progressed, when online physio is suitable.
4. Trust: clinician credentials, professional standards, privacy, calm patient experience.
5. Booking: prices, packages, who should book, what happens after booking.

## Production Rules

- Format: vertical 9:16, 1080x1920.
- Target length: 20 to 45 seconds.
- Hook: first 2 seconds must name the problem or promise.
- Captions: always use on-screen captions.
- Visual style: bright, uncluttered, clinical but warm.
- Avoid dense medical diagrams unless they are easy to understand.
- Add the video watermark in one lower corner only when it does not cover captions or platform UI.
- Use UK English.

## Safety Rules

- General education only.
- Do not diagnose viewers.
- Do not promise cures, guaranteed pain relief, or fixed recovery timelines.
- Avoid fear-based claims.
- Do not use patient stories, images, DMs, or comments unless explicit documented consent exists.
- For severe, worsening, traumatic, neurological, bladder/bowel, chest pain, fever, unexplained weight loss, or other red-flag symptoms, advise urgent medical support.
- Personal medical questions in comments or DMs should be redirected to booking an assessment or contacting an appropriate healthcare professional.

## Weekly Review

Every Friday, record:

- Views
- Average watch time or retention where available
- Likes
- Comments
- Shares
- Saves
- Profile visits
- Website clicks
- Booking page clicks
- Bookings or enquiries
- DMs that need response

Use results to decide:

- Repeat: topics that get saves, shares, comments, or bookings.
- Refine: topics with views but no action.
- Stop: topics with low retention and no engagement after two attempts.
