### Instagram Reel

Hook:
Restarting movement after pain? Start smaller than you think.

Script:
If you have been inactive, injured, or nervous after pain, do not jump straight into a hard workout. Start with a simple check: can you move gently, breathe comfortably, and feel okay later the same day? Begin with short, easy activity, then build gradually. If symptoms are severe, spreading, linked with new weakness, chest pain, bladder or bowel changes, fever, or trauma, seek urgent medical advice. For a plan that fits you, book an online PhysioOnClick assessment.
